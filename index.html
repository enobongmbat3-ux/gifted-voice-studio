FAQ

Quick answers.

What kind of audio works best?

Clear speech with little background noise. For the reference, 10–60 seconds of a single speaker works well. Music or overlapping voices reduce quality.

Will the timing and emotion stay the same?

The goal is to keep the original dialogue, pacing and emotion as closely as the underlying model allows. Results vary with audio quality.

Are my files uploaded when I select them?

No. Previews play locally in your browser. Files are sent to the conversion service only when you press Convert.

Why does it say the service isn't connected?

This app never fakes results. A real voice-conversion model must be connected on the server — see the README for setup.

Can I use any voice?

Only voices you own or have permission to use.
